# AI Prototype Studio Positioning

## Mission

Help founders and businesses transform ideas into working AI-powered products quickly.

## Core Belief

Great ideas should not die because building technology is too slow, expensive, or complicated.

## Position

AI Prototype Studio is a rapid AI product development studio focused on turning concepts into functional prototypes.

We combine:
- Product thinking
- AI development
- Rapid prototyping
- User-focused design

## Target Customers

### Primary

Non-technical founders who need to validate ideas.

### Secondary

- Startup teams
- Researchers
- Creators
- Small businesses

## Differentiator

We don't just build software.

We help define what should be built.

Process:

Idea → Prototype → Feedback → Launch
