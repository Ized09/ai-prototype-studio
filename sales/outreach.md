# AI Prototype Studio Outreach System

## Objective

Create meaningful conversations with founders, creators, and businesses that need AI product development support.

---

# Target Groups

## 1. Founders

Profile:
- Early-stage startup founders
- Non-technical founders
- Solo founders validating ideas

Message angle:

"I help founders turn ideas into working AI prototypes quickly."

---

## 2. Agencies

Profile:
- Design agencies
- Marketing agencies
- Development agencies

Message angle:

"I provide AI prototyping support when your clients need technical execution."

---

## 3. Creators

Profile:
- Content creators
- Educators
- Community builders

Message angle:

"I help transform audience ideas into AI-powered products."

---

# Outreach Principles

Do:

- Personalize every message
- Mention specific work
- Lead with curiosity
- Offer value

Avoid:

- Spam
- Generic AI pitches
- Over-selling

---

# First Message Template

Hi [Name],

I noticed you're working on [specific thing].

I build AI prototypes that help founders validate ideas quickly. Recently I built projects like MetaFix, an AI royalty health platform, and AOS, an AI founder operating system.

I would love to learn more about what you're building and see if there is any way I can help.

---

# Tracking

Every outreach attempt should record:

- Person
- Company
- Date contacted
- Response
- Next action
