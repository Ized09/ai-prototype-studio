# Discovery Call Framework

## Goal

Understand the problem before discussing solutions.

---

## Questions

### Problem

What are you trying to build?

Why now?

What happens if this problem remains unsolved?

---

### Users

Who experiences this problem?

How do they solve it currently?

---

### Product

What would success look like?

What is the smallest version that proves this idea?

---

### Resources

Timeline?

Budget?

Technical requirements?

---

# Outcome

Every call ends with:

- Clear next step
- Timeline
- Deliverables
- Decision maker
